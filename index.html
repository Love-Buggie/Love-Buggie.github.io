✦ about me

Arizona-grown, mostly self-taught, endlessly curious.

I graduated Cum Laude from Arizona State University in 2022 with a bachelor's in Digital Culture, and I've been building interactive things ever since — games, VR experiences, educational STEM apps, 3D city twins.

Most of my day-to-day is UI, player interaction, and general development: the parts users actually touch. I picked up 3D modeling, animation, VFX and project management along the way, which means I can usually take a project from a rough idea to something polished.

I've also led a guild of animators and modelers, and I care a lot about making teams where people feel safe sharing half-finished work. Next up: games or STEM education, as a developer or a lead.
